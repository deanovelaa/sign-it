Sign It

Draw your signature. Export it as a clean SVG that scales anywhere.

Everything happens in this tab. There is no server, no upload and no tracking — your signature never leaves your device.

Export

Download PNG

The SVG export is cropped tightly to your ink and carries a viewBox with no fixed width or height, so it scales to fit wherever you put it. PNG is the fallback for tools that cannot take vectors.

An image, not a legal signature — this tool does not sign documents or create an audit trail. MIT licensed. Built on perfect-freehand, vendored into the repo so the page works offline.
